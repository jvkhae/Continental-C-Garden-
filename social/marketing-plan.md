# The Continental Hotel — Social Media Marketing Plan

> Placeholder framework for general hotel brand awareness (not just the C Garden restaurant). Swap the bracketed specifics — handles, city, real amenities, photography — for the real thing whenever you have them; the structure below doesn't need to change.

## 1. Goals

- Build awareness of **The Continental** as a hotel brand, not just "the place with C Garden."
- Drive two measurable actions: direct bookings and foot traffic to C Garden.
- Establish a consistent visual identity across platforms (see `../social/` graphics and the palette below).

## 2. Audience

| Segment | What they care about |
|---|---|
| Local diners | C Garden's food, atmosphere, easy reason to visit without booking a room |
| Leisure travelers | Quiet/boutique feel, location, photogenic rooms & common areas |
| Business travelers | Reliability, wifi/workspace, quick access to dining without leaving the hotel |
| Event planners | Event/meeting space, catering tie-in with C Garden |

## 3. Brand voice & visual identity

- **Voice:** understated, warm, a little literary — short sentences, no exclamation points, no hard-sell language. ("Where the city slows down," not "BEST DEALS NOW!!")
- **Palette:** deep green `#1f3b2c`, gold `#b08d3e`, cream `#f7f4ee`, ink `#23291f` — lifted directly from the existing `index.html` menu site so every asset feels like one property.
- **Type:** Cormorant Garamond (serif, headlines) + Inter (sans, body/labels), matching the menu site.
- **Motif:** a simple gold botanical line-drawing (branch/leaves), tying "C Garden" into the wider hotel identity.

## 4. Platforms

- **Facebook** — primary for the poster/cover-image format, events, local community reach, older demographic of leisure travelers.
- **Instagram** — square posts, Stories, reels of the space/food; best for photogenic amenities and C Garden dishes.
- **(Optional) TikTok** — short behind-the-scenes / staff / food prep clips, only once photo/video assets exist.

## 5. Content pillars

1. **Brand & atmosphere** — the hotel as a place, not a room type. ("A quiet address in a loud city.")
2. **C Garden dining** — food, the QR menu, daily specials.
3. **Amenities & experience** — rooms, rooftop, garden, spa/wellness if applicable.
4. **Events & offers** — seasonal promotions, private events, direct-booking incentives.
5. **Social proof** — guest reviews/UGC once available (never fabricate reviews or numbers).

## 6. Posting cadence (starting point)

| Platform | Frequency | Format mix |
|---|---|---|
| Facebook | 3×/week | 1 poster/cover-style graphic, 1 dining post, 1 offer/event or reshare |
| Instagram feed | 4–5×/week | Mix of the 3 content pillars above, rotate weekly |
| Instagram Stories | Daily when there's something to show | Behind-the-scenes, polls, menu highlights |

## 7. Sample 4-week calendar (repeat/adapt monthly)

| Week | Facebook | Instagram |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Brand poster ("Where the City Slows Down") | Welcome/brand post + Stories teaser |
| 2 | C Garden dining post, "scan the menu" CTA | Dish close-ups, QR-menu Story highlight |
| 3 | Amenities post (rooms/garden/rooftop) | Amenity carousel, guest-eye-view Stories |
| 4 | Offer/event or review highlight (real content only) | Recap Reel / top-post repost |

## 8. Measurement

- Track link clicks to the booking page and to the C Garden menu QR/URL separately.
- Watch reach, saves, and comments as brand-awareness signals (not just likes).
- Revisit this plan monthly once real photography, offers, and guest content exist — right now it's built entirely on placeholder copy so it's ready the moment real details are dropped in.
